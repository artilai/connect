Rules:

- `src/` has three layers and the entry point. `main.ts` reads the environment and brings the built-in channels. `domains/<area>/` holds one area each. `app/` assembles the server: each domain's service joins the others in `services.ts`, its routes in `http.ts`, its tools in `mcp.ts` and its commands in `cli.ts`. `platform/` holds what every domain shares and nothing area-specific.
- Every domain has the same files, only the ones it needs. `schema.ts` holds its API shapes in zod, its record types and the `toX` presenters. `repository.ts` holds SQLite and nothing else. `service.ts` holds the use cases and every rule, and is what other domains and the adapters use. `routes.ts`, `tools.ts`, `commands.ts` and `middleware.ts` are the HTTP, MCP, CLI and Hono adapters: each reads its input, calls a service and presents the result. A file named for one of the domain's own concepts, like `admission.ts` or `stream.ts`, sits beside them, and `<area>.test.ts` tests the domain.
- `artil/server-architecture` in `.config/lint/index.ts` checks the layers on every lint: adapters never touch a repository, services and repositories know no transport, a domain reaches another only through its `service.ts` or `schema.ts`, and nothing imports upward. Fix what it finds rather than working around it.
